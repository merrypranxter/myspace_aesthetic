# Comment Reaction GIFs

## What this category is
Small, emotionally explicit graphics made to be dropped into Myspace comment threads.
They are less about composition and more about **social signaling at a glance**.

Typical dimensions:
- 120–320 px wide for banners
- 80–180 px for stickers
- 1:1 or 4:3 for character loops

## Core forms
- **Name-callout stamps**: “HEY ASHLEY!!” with glitter burst behind text.
- **Mood reaction loops**: blink/wink/cry/laugh doll or anime cutout with halo.
- **Support badges**: “ILY GIRL”, “STAY STRONG”, “MISS U”, “HAPPY BDAY”.
- **Drama subtweets (indirect)**: “SOME PPL FAKE”, “WOW OK”, “DON’T START”.
- **Guestbook-era signoffs**: “thx 4 da comment”, “returning the luv”, “xoxo”.

## Text behavior rules
- Keep copy short (2–7 words preferred).
- Favor uppercase + intentional shorthand (U, UR, 4EVER, LYLAS).
- Use stacked emphasis punctuation (`!!!`, `...`, `?!`).
- Include at least one emotional keyword per asset: `love`, `miss`, `lol`, `ugh`, `bff`, `drama`, `cute`.

## Visual stack
1. Loud gradient or dark stage plate.
2. Bevel/chrome/gel lettering.
3. Glitter pass OR blink pass (not both at max intensity).
4. White outline or pink glow around key subject.
5. Optional tiny icon confetti (hearts, stars, lips, skulls).

## Motion profile
- Loop length: 0.6–1.8s.
- 2–6 frame economy is preferred for authenticity.
- Fast alternation reads as “comment energy”; avoid smooth modern tweening.

## Drift warnings
- Too editorial, too clean kerning, or modern flat emoji look = wrong era.
- Long paragraph text on the graphic = wrong use case.
- Photoreal shadows and cinematic grading = wrong material language.

## Repair moves
- Reduce resolution, add 1px outline jitter, reintroduce crunchy compression.
- Replace sentence-style copy with slogan chunks.
- Push one tacky luxury effect (glitter/chrome/gel) 30–40% harder.
